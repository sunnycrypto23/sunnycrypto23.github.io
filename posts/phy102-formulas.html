Master Quick Reference: All Units

A consolidated, unsummarized sheet of all key formulas from Units 1–5 for quick revision before exams.

★ EXAM TIP: Memorize the units for each quantity. Dimensional analysis can often help you eliminate wrong answers in CBT questions!

Unit 1: Electrostatics & Electric Fields

QuantityFormulaUnit
Coulomb’s LawF = k\|q₁q₂\| / r²N
Electric Field (point)E = kq / r² = q / (4πε₀r²)N/C or V/m
Electric PotentialV = kQ / rV
Work / Potential EnergyW = q∆VJ
Gauss’s Law∯ E · dA = Q_enc / ε₀N·m²/C
Electric FluxΦ = EA cosθN·m²/C
Capacitance (General)C = Q / \|∆V\|F
Parallel-plate CC = ε₀A / dF
Cylindrical CC = 2πε₀L / ln(b/a)F
Spherical CC = 4πε₀ab / (b − a)F
Dipole momentp = qdC·m
Energy in capacitorU = ½CV² = ½QV = ½Q²/CJ
Energy densityu_E = ½ε₀E²J/m³

Unit 3: Magnetism & Magnetic Fields

QuantityFormulaUnit
Lorentz forceF = q(E + v × B)N

Unit 4: Electromagnetic Induction

QuantityFormulaUnit
Faraday’s lawℰ = −N(dΦ/dt)V
Magnetic fluxΦ = BA cosθWb
